Project Summary

3D Platformer built in Unity 6

SYMBIOSIS is an independently developed third-person 3D platformer built in Unity 6, with a focus on high-speed movement, swinging, and combat. Inspired by the Sonic franchise, SpeedRunners, Spider-Man, and Venom, the game follows a character infected by an alien parasite that grants powerful abilities while gradually damaging his mind. This summer, I began rebuilding the project from scratch, dedicating most of my development time to redesigning its movement and physics systems. The current build introduces more responsive traversal, consistent grapple mechanics, expanded combat options, and improved camera and player-feedback systems while preserving the original concept and identity of the game.

Project History

Development Over Time

The original SYMBIOSIS grew from a sophomore-year classroom project into a two-level playable demo. It featured hand-drawn animated cutscenes, movement-focused level design, lock-on combat, advanced UI and scene transitions, and a Madness system that rewarded parasite abilities with temporary strength at the cost of hallucinations and increased vulnerability. Its fully manual, physics-based swinging system was ambitious, but difficult to control and often interrupted the fast-paced movement the game was designed around. The current rebuild preserves the original world, narrative, Madness system, and strongest gameplay ideas while redesigning traversal around a consistent grapple angle and guided pendulum arc.

Combat Design

Directional Fighting

The left and right arrow keys throw punches toward different openings while the down arrow blocks incoming attacks. Each action changes Goku's visual state, checks for a collision, applies damage, and updates both health and the win/lose state.

Player loop Predict Piccolo's head position, input a punch direction, then block the counterattack.

Enemy Systems

Punches & Ki Attacks

Piccolo head changes position and attacks after randomized delays. Flashing hands telegraph normal punches, while a growing energy effect signals a charged ki attack that deals heavier damage unless the player blocks in time.

Attack selection Each decision chooses between a standard punch and a less frequent ki attack.

Sprite Artwork

Character States

Separate idle, punch, block, damage, expression, hand, and effect sprites are swapped and layered at runtime to communicate every combat state inside the small canvas.
