About Me

I am a senior studying Games, Interactive Media, and Mobile Technology at Boise State University. As a multidisciplinary designer and developer, I create games, visual identities, interfaces, and digital experiences that combine thoughtful interaction with strong visual direction.

My work spans graphic design, UI/UX, gameplay programming, web development, 3D modeling, and animation. This range allows me to approach projects from both creative and technical perspectives—developing the systems behind an experience while also shaping how it looks, feels, and communicates.

I am especially interested in work where design, technology, and storytelling come together. Whether collaborating with a team or developing a project independently, I aim to create work that is visually distinctive, intuitive to use, and carefully considered from concept through execution.
